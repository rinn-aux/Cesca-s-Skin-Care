Skin safety: Avoid retinoids during pregnancy or when trying to conceive. Follow the labels and stop actives if your skin stings, peels or becomes irritated. After dermaplaning, keep the routine gentle until fully comfortable; this app schedules a conservative three-day buffer either side. Clay masks may contain exfoliating acids depending on the variant, so confirm your exact label. Sunscreen is important every morning and should be reapplied outdoors.
